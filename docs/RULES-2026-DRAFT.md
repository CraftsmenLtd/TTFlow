# Table Tennis Tournament 2026: Rules (DRAFT)

> **Status: draft for organising committee verification.** Adapted from the 2025 rules. Items marked **[Confirm]** need a committee decision. Once approved, this text becomes version 1 of the in-app Rules page.

## Changes from 2025

| Topic | 2025 | 2026 (proposed) |
|---|---|---|
| Reschedules | Up to 2 per player | **One per match in total**, strictly enforced (§7) |
| Group sizes | Fixed groups of 3 with "dummy" slots | Balanced groups of 3–4 decided after registration closes; byes instead of dummy players (§3) |
| Tiebreaks | Points → matches won → set difference → point difference | Points → results among tied players → game difference → point difference → drawing of lots (§5) |
| Late arrival | Not defined | 10-minute grace period, then walkover **[Confirm]** (§6) |
| Draw | Manual files | Random computer draw, published in the app, reproducible (§2) |

---

## 1. Events and eligibility
- Events: **Men's Singles**, **Men's Doubles**, **Women's Singles**.
- Open to Craftsmen employees who register in the app before the registration deadline.
- Men play **both** Singles and Doubles. Women play Singles.
- Withdrawing after the draw follows §8.

## 2. The draw
- Groups (Singles) and teams (Doubles) are drawn **randomly by the app** after registration closes, then published in the app.
- Each draw is recorded so it can be reproduced and verified.
- The committee may redraw **only before any match in that event has been played**, and must announce it.

## 3. Format
**Men's Singles**
- Players are split into groups of 3 (or 4, depending on numbers) and play a **round robin**: everyone plays everyone in the group.
- The **top 2** of each group go to the **knockout** stage (e.g. Round of 16 → Quarter-finals → Semi-finals → Final, depending on numbers).
- Group winners meet runners-up from another group in the first knockout round. Two players from the same group are placed in opposite halves of the bracket.
- If the number of qualifiers isn't a power of 2, the best group winners get a **bye** to the next round.

**Men's Doubles**
- Teams of two are formed by a **random lottery** among registered men and published in the app.
- If there's an odd number of players, the committee decides how to handle the remaining player. **[Confirm]**
- Groups, round robin and knockout work as in Men's Singles.

**Women's Singles**
- With **6 or fewer** players: a single round robin, then the **top 2 play the final**.
- With more players: the same format as Men's Singles.

**Plate (optional) [Confirm]**
- Players finishing 3rd in their group may play a separate Plate knockout.

## 4. Match rules
- Matches are **best of 3 games**. Semi-finals and finals may be **best of 5**. **[Confirm]**
- A game is won by the first to **11 points**. At **10–10**, play continues until one side leads by **2 points**.
- Service changes every 2 points, and every point from 10–10. Players change ends after each game, and at 5 points in the deciding game.
- Doubles: serve diagonally from the right half-court to the right half-court, and partners hit alternately.
- Points not covered here follow the basic ITTF Laws of Table Tennis.

## 5. Points and ranking (group stage)
- **Win = 2 points, loss = 0 points.**
- Ties are broken in this order:
  1. Total points
  2. Results **among the tied players only** (points, then games ratio, then points ratio)
  3. Overall **game difference** (games won − games lost)
  4. Overall **point difference** (points won − points lost)
  5. **Drawing of lots** by the committee, recorded in the app

## 6. Scheduling and attendance
- Fixtures and times are published in the app ("Today's matches" and "My matches").
- Players must be at the table at the scheduled time.
- A player or team not ready **10 minutes** after the scheduled time loses by **walkover**. **[Confirm]**
- A walkover is recorded as a 2–0 win (11–0 in each game).

## 7. Reschedule policy (strict)
- **Each match can be rescheduled only once.** Either side may use it, and after that the match can't be rescheduled again.
- Request it in the app **before the scheduled start time**, with a reason.
- The committee approves it and sets the new time. The request also notifies your opponent.
- If either side can't play at the rescheduled time, that side **loses by walkover** (provided the opponent is ready).
- If both sides are unavailable, the committee decides the outcome.

## 8. Withdrawal and disqualification
- A player who withdraws or is disqualified after the draw forfeits all remaining matches by walkover. Matches already played still count.
- The committee may disqualify any participant for misconduct, repeated delays, rule violations or unsportsmanlike behaviour.

## 9. Scoring and disputes
- Scores are recorded in the app by an appointed **scorer**.
- Players should check the final score before leaving the table.
- Disputes must be raised with the committee **within 30 minutes** of the match ending. **[Confirm]**
- Every score change is logged.

## 10. Authority
- The match referee's or scorer's decision on the table is final.
- The committee may make any decision the tournament needs, and all participants must follow it.

## 11. Conduct
- Players must behave professionally at all times and respect the spirit of fair play.
- **Zero tolerance:** harassment of, or disrespect towards, referees, scorers, volunteers or players is strictly prohibited, and any unsportsmanlike conduct will lead to immediate action.
